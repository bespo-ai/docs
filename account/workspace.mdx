Your Script.it workspace is where everything lives — your scripts, sessions, integrations, and account settings. When you sign up, Script.it creates a workspace for you automatically. Everything you build is stored there and persists between sessions, so you can return to any script or conversation at any time.

## Signing up

To create an account, go to [app.script.it/login](https://app.script.it/login). You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. No credit card is required to get started — your account comes with free included credits right away.

<Note>
  New accounts are on the free plan by default. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time from **Settings → Billing**. See [Billing, credits, and plans](/account/billing) for details.
</Note>

## What a workspace contains

Once you're signed in, everything you build lives inside your workspace:

- **Scripts** — the automation workflows you create, each with its own blocks, source files, and trigger configuration.
- **Sessions** — chat conversations with the AI agent, each with its own separate workspace.
- **Integrations** — third-party tools you've connected, such as Slack, Gmail, or Notion.
- **Triggers** — rules that fire your scripts automatically on a schedule or in response to external events.

Your workspace data is automatically backed up, so you don't need to worry about losing your scripts or session history.

## Accessing settings

To manage your account, open **Settings** from the workspace navigation. From there you can:

- View and edit your profile information
- Check your credit balance and usage history
- Upgrade your plan or manage billing
- Review connected integrations and revoke access if needed

The pages in this section cover billing and security in detail.
